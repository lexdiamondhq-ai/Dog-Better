Affiliate disclosure

Updated September 17, 2026.

How we make money

Dog Better is a free iOS app with a paid Premium subscription. We also earn a commission when you buy something through certain product links in the app or on this site. That is an affiliate relationship. It does not change the price you pay.

Amazon Associates

As an Amazon Associate, Dog Better earns from qualifying purchases. Dog Better is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

Shop links, treat-scanner buy links, and some partner cards open Amazon search or product pages. If you order a qualifying item after using one of those links, Amazon may pay us a commission.

Other partners

If we add pet insurance or other referral partners, those links will be marked as partner listings in the app and listed here. Local business cards in the app currently open a plain Maps search; nobody pays for them.

What we will not do

We do not put affiliate links inside Emergency, symptom triage, or the clinic pack. We do not recommend a product only because it pays more. The dog's profile (size, allergies, the label) comes first. Premium hides partner cards entirely.
