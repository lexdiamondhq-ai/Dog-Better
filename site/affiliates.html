Affiliate disclosure

Updated September 2026.

How we make money

Dog Better is a paid iOS app with a free layer. We also earn a commission when you buy something through certain product links in the app or on this site. That is an affiliate relationship. It does not change the price you pay.

Amazon Associates

Dog Better is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

Shop links, treat-scanner “buy” links, and some partner slots open Amazon search or product pages with our tracking tag. If you order a qualifying item after using that link, Amazon may pay us a commission.

Other partners

We may also link to pet insurance, local walkers, grooms, or other services under a separate referral agreement. Those links are marked as partner listings in the app.

What we will not do

We do not put affiliate links inside Emergency, clinic triage, or the vet summary. We do not recommend a product only because it pays more. The dog’s profile (size, allergies, the label) comes first.
