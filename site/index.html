The roster for your dog.

Dog Better is an iOS app for the person holding the leash. It keeps meals, walks, meds, and the care sheet in one place, and it checks a treat barcode before you share it.

Contact support How we earn

What the app does

  • Today: a short duty list for this dog, not a feed.
  • Treat scanner: point at a barcode or paste the ingredient line. We look it up and flag hazards for this dog’s weight and allergies.
  • Care sheet: feeding, the vet, and the do-not list, ready to send to a sitter.
  • Walks, parks, and trails: start a walk from a map of dog areas near you.
  • Look: a photo of a paw, a bag, or a label. AI, not a vet.

Who this is for

Households with one dog or a few. Sitters who need the sheet. Anyone in an aisle wondering if this bag is safe to share.

It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace a veterinarian. If a dog is in distress, call a clinic.
