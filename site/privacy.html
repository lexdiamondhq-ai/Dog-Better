Privacy policy

Draft, September 2026. Written to be honest. Not a substitute for a lawyer’s review.

What we collect

Your email and sign-in identity. What you log about your dog: profile, meals, symptoms, weights, treat checks, photos, and places you report on. Device information when you contact support.

Why we collect it

To show you patterns in your dog’s health and behaviour, to build the care sheet and vet summaries you ask for, and to keep your account secure. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Photos and records

Photos and health records are private to your account. They are only shared when you choose to send a care sheet or summary. Profile pictures are stored in a bucket that can be linked, so they can be shown to people you share with.

Location

Location is used to show the walk map and to find nearby vets, dog parks, trails, and patios. Crowd reports you post are tied to the place, not to you, when shown to others.

Health inferences

Guidance in the app is general triage and never a diagnosis. It is generated from what you log; we do not share it with insurers or third parties.

Where data lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL and file storage) in the United States, protected by row-level security so only your account can read your rows.

Your rights

Export everything at any time from Settings. Request deletion from Settings and we remove your account and all its data after confirming by email. Questions: support@dogbetter.app.

Children

Dog Better is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.
